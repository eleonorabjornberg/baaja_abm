## Check against BLM solar right-of-way outcomes (MLRS)

9.6% of resolved BLM solar development grant applications were authorized (n=741; the rest closed). Model, current law: 8.7% of solar projects with a federal nexus reach operation (all solar: 12.6%). Intake (filing -> NEPA notice) is measured from MLRS filing dates: median 10.5 months (n=60; 2010-18 filings 14.4, 2019-26 9.4); each project draws from that spread.
