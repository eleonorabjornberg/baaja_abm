# Simulation 3: grid access

All data-based BAAJA provisions are on in every row; only the Sec.2106 study-time cut varies. In the Senate-passed text Sec.2106 applies to Transmission Organizations (RTO/ISO markets) only, so the cut applies to projects in the six FERC-jurisdictional RTOs/ISOs (CAISO, ISO-NE, MISO, NYISO, PJM, SPP); ERCOT (outside FERC's jurisdiction for these rules), the Southeast and the non-ISO West are unaffected. Sec.2110 applies to all public-utility transmission providers and sets no target (central 0%, range 0-5%). For Sec.2106 the data give a bracket, not a point: no measured shortening from past queue reforms (floor 0%), and ERCOT's pace as a ceiling: ERCOT 23 vs CAISO/MISO 40 months from request to signed agreement (2015-19 requests), a 42% cut. Reaching it fully would also need energy-only interconnection, which the bill does not mandate. The cut applies to study months after the rules take effect (central month 36 = late 2029).
