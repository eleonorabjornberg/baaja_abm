# Simulation 3: grid access

All data-based BAAJA provisions are on in every row; only the interconnection study-time cut varies. Sec.2110 sets no target (central 0%, range 0-5%). For Sec.2106 the data give a bracket, not a point: no measured shortening from past queue reforms (floor 0%), and ERCOT's pace as a ceiling: ERCOT 23 vs CAISO/MISO 40 months from request to signed agreement (2015-19 requests), a 42% cut. Reaching it fully would also need energy-only interconnection, which the bill does not mandate. The cut applies to study months after the rules take effect (central month 36 = late 2029).
