# Simulation 3: grid access

All data-based BAAJA provisions are on in every row; only the Sec.2106 study-time cut varies. In the Senate-passed text Sec.2106 applies to Transmission Organizations (RTO/ISO markets) only, so the cut applies to projects in ISO markets; non-ISO regions (Southeast, most of the West) are unaffected. ERCOT is counted as an ISO market here, although it is outside FERC's jurisdiction for these rules, so the GW below are somewhat overstated until the regional version (Phase 1) separates it. Sec.2110 applies to all public-utility transmission providers and sets no target (central 0%, range 0-5%). For Sec.2106 the data give a bracket, not a point: no measured shortening from past queue reforms (floor 0%), and ERCOT's pace as a ceiling: ERCOT 23 vs CAISO/MISO 40 months from request to signed agreement (2015-19 requests), a 42% cut. Reaching it fully would also need energy-only interconnection, which the bill does not mandate. The cut applies to study months after the rules take effect (central month 36 = late 2029).
