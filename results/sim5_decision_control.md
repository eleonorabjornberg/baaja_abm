## Not modelled with data

Sec.2105 (no ROFR) and Sec.2104 change who builds transmission, not when generation connects; the model has no measured link from transmission sponsorship to generator completion. Sec.2108 concerns behind-the-meter resources, outside the interconnection queue. These are listed, not simulated.
