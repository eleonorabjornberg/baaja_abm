## Not modelled with data

Sec.2105 (no ROFR) and Sec.2104 change who builds transmission, not when generation connects; the model has no measured link from transmission sponsorship to generator completion. Sec.2108 concerns behind-the-meter resources, outside the interconnection queue. Sec.2107 assigns transmission costs to computational loads (data centers) and Sec.2114 requires data collection on them: they change who pays and what is reported, not generator timing. These are listed, not simulated; the data-center build-out enters only through the 2025 request regime.
